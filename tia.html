Based on the TIA guidelines and the application parameters provided, here is the structured configuration to add Math (6–8), ELA (6–8), Science (6–8), and Fine Arts (6–8) to your district's Weighting Tab and Eligible Teachers Tab[cite: 2].

1. Weighting Tab Configurations

Because Math, ELA, and Science (6–8) share similar assessment/growth structures (typically using standardized pre/post-tests or VAM/SLOs), they can be grouped together if using the same weights[cite: 2]. Fine Arts (6–8) uses a portfolio-based student growth measure, which requires its own distinct category and table[cite: 2, 3].

3. Application Readiness Checklist Checks

  • Multiple Assessments: If Math, ELA, and Science use different test vendors or different growth target rules (e.g., NWEA MAP for Math vs. STAAR VAM for ELA), separate them into individual Category numbers on the Weighting Tab[cite: 2].
  • Support Teachers: If any interventionists, inclusion, or dyslexia teachers support these 6–8 courses, set their category to "Multiple" on the Eligible Teachers Tab if they span more than one category[cite: 2].
